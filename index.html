03 // What Was Involved End-to-End

System Architecture, Hardware, Training & Software Stack

Every technical layer built, integrated, trained, and validated across neural architecture design, multi-camera perception, GPU systems engineering, simulation digital twins, and physical UART robot control.

Figure 1 — Two-Stage RoboMamba-DINO-ACT Pipeline (click any diagram to enlarge): Dual 224×224 RGB streams → Frozen DINOv2 ViT-B/14 (256 tokens/cam) → 4×4 Spatial Adaptive Pooling (32 visual tokens) + Joint State + CVAE Latent → 6-Layer Mamba SSM Encoder → ACT Cross-Attention Decoder (16-step 6-DoF action chunk).
01 · Visual Perception

Frozen Meta DINOv2 + 4×4 Spatial Adaptive Pooling

Standard ResNet-18 backbones apply global average pooling that discards 2D coordinate geometry, causing severe sim-to-real failure during 3D box placement. We use frozen self-supervised DINOv2 ViT-B/14 (86.58M frozen params) to extract 256 geometric patch tokens per camera (768-dim), then reshape into a 16×16 spatial grid and apply 2D adaptive pooling to 4×4:

512 raw tokens (2×256) → 32 spatial tokens (16× reduction, preserving 2D layout)
02 · Sequence Backbone

6-Layer RoboMamba Selective State-Space Encoder

Replaces quadratic O(L²) Transformer self-attention with a 6-layer Selective State-Space Model (Mamba) operating in linear O(L) compute and constant O(1) recurrent memory. Concatenates 32 pooled visual tokens, 1 projected 6-DoF proprioception token, and 1 CVAE latent token into a compact 34-token sequence.

d_model = 512, d_state = 16, expand E = 2, d_conv = 4 (22.37M trainable params)
03 · Trajectory Generation

ACT Query Decoder, CVAE & Temporal Ensembling

A DETR-style Transformer decoder uses K = 16 sinusoidal action queries to predict a 16-step 6-DoF trajectory chunk in one parallel pass. Trained with unified continuous L1 loss + KL divergence regularization (β = 10.0). At inference, the 12.65M CVAE encoder is omitted (z = 0), leaving only 27.00M active parameters smoothed via exponential temporal ensembling.

w_i = exp(−0.01 · i) · Smooth closed-loop joint targets without boundary jitter
04 · Physical Robot HITL

6-DoF SO-101 Leader–Follower & Dual Cameras

  • Manipulator: 5 revolute joints (waist, shoulder, elbow, wrist pitch, wrist roll) + 1 continuous parallel gripper.
  • Actuation & Bus: Feetech STS3215 serial bus servos with 12-bit absolute magnetic encoders over 1,000,000-baud half-duplex UART (/dev/ttyACM0).
  • Sensors & Data: Synchronized static side + in-hand wrist USB cameras (640×480 @ 30 FPS); recorded 300 physical teleoperated episodes (76,634 AV1 frames) in LeRobot v3.0 format.
05 · Simulation Digital Twin

NVIDIA Isaac Sim 5.1 / Isaac Lab (LeIsaac)

  • Environment: Custom tabletop task LeIsaac-SO101-CustomTask-v0 picking a 35 mm red cube into a 100×100×40 mm grey box.
  • Physics & Rendering: PhysX 5.0 contact dynamics + ray-traced overhead front and wrist virtual cameras; 300 simulated demonstration episodes.
  • Evaluation Harness: Automated multi-seed benchmark across seeds 42, 100, 2024 (150 trials/model) tracking 4 Cartesian sub-goal milestones.
06 · Compute & MLOps

RTX 5090 Training, RTX 3080 Profiling & Docker

  • Training: 200,000 gradient steps on NVIDIA RTX 5090 (32 GB VRAM), batch size 64, AdamW + Cosine LR, FP16 AMP, and contiguous in-RAM trajectory caching for zero disk I/O stalls.
  • Profiling: Exact GPU kernel timing via torch.cuda.Event & NVML on RTX 3080 (10 GB VRAM).
  • Deployment: Dockerized inference stack + Hugging Face Hub Safetensors auto-export + pre-flight hardware diagnostic suite.

05 // Systems Debugging & Operational Safety

Real-World Hardware Troubleshooting & Safety Engineering

Bridging neural policies from simulation to physical serial servos and operating reliably on physical lab hardware required solving concrete hardware-software edge cases.

Root-Cause Fix #1 · Physical Hardware

Solving −21.77σ Out-of-Distribution Servo Jump

During initial physical SO-101 tests, wrist_roll snapped violently by +136.4° at startup. Telemetry profiling revealed Feetech STS3215 180° horn periodicity caused resting encoders to read −153.8° against a training distribution of +2.97° ± 7.20° (a −21.77σ OOD input). Implemented a 2-second smooth linear auto-homing routine to align all 6 joints to the exact dataset manifold prior to policy loop activation.

Root-Cause Fix #2 · Sim-to-Real Bridge

Gripper Release Calibration & Python 3.11 AST Refactor

Diagnosed why early simulation rollouts grasped and transported the cube at 100% yet failed to release over the box: a legacy linear scaling heuristic compressed predicted 35°–45° opening angles below the release threshold. Restoring uncorrupted continuous [0°, 60.76°] pass-through unlocked full task completion. Also refactored PEP 695 generics across 150+ modules for zero-error compatibility with Isaac Sim's Python 3.11 runtime.

Hardware Operations · Safety & Reliability

6-Stage Pre-Flight Diagnostic & Signed Risk Register

Engineered demo_preflight_check.py to validate CUDA memory, checkpoint integrity, dataset z-score normalizer tensors, temporal ensembling buffers, and 30 Hz loop latency before every physical hardware run. Authored the formal University of Bristol SO-101 Safety Risk Register (5 V USB low-torque compliance, 20% velocity cap, 1 m exclusion barrier, and E-stop watchdog) signed off by Supervisor Mark Hansen.
