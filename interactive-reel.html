Two cameras give 512 patch tokens. Pooling each 16 × 16 grid down to 4 × 4 throws away 94 % of them before anything expensive happens.

Everything downstream — the state-space scan, the cross-attention, the memory bill — is priced against a sequence of 33.

Temporal ensembling

Sixteen
impressions,
one action.

Chunks overlap, so the action for any timestep has already been predicted up to sixteen times from sixteen different observations. Averaging them by age is registration — the same problem a two-colour press has.

w = exp(−k · age),  k = 0.01

Read the constant

At k = 0.01 the oldest prediction still carries 0.861 of the newest one's weight — in practice a flat average, not a decay. A knob worth ablating.
